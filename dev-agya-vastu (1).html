About

Three disciplines.
One practice.

For generations, Vastu Shastra has guided how a home should meet the sun, how a threshold should be placed, how a room should hold the people living in it. Every project we take on starts there — with a plot, a direction, and that older discipline of reading a space before building on it.

We test that reading against measurable data: sun-path and daylight study, soil and drainage behaviour, wind and ventilation, structural load. Where tradition and measurement agree, we build with confidence. Where they don't, we work out why before a single wall goes up.

Only then do we design the building itself — structure, material, proportion, light — the way any serious architecture practice would, for a client building in the present, not four centuries ago.

We work with individuals building their first home, companies planning their next office, and developers laying out a floor or an entire building. The scale changes. The method doesn't.

Old order. New proof. New form.

Approach

How a project actually moves.

01

Classical reading

We map the plot or structure against Vastu principles — direction, entry, elemental zones — using methods carried down through generations of practice.

02

Scientific verification

We check that reading against site science: sun-path and light, soil and structural data, airflow and climate. Tradition has to hold up under measurement.

03

Contemporary design

Only once both agree do we draw — layouts, elevations, materials — built for how people actually live and work today.
