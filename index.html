How to check house Vastu without demolition?

Begin with a correctly oriented floor plan and review the room use, circulation, light and practical constraints. Recommendations depend on the actual property; no structural change should be made without the responsible professional's review.

What is the best main entrance direction as per 32 Vastu zones?

There is no one-size-fits-all direction. A 32-zone review needs a verified north mark, the measured position along the plot edge and the full plan, alongside access, privacy and local requirements.

Can you provide online Vastu map analysis?

Yes. Share an oriented floor plan, property location and your key questions. We confirm whether the information supports an online review or whether a site visit is appropriate.

Do you review commercial and factory layouts?

Yes. Reviews can consider orientation, access and functional adjacencies, while engineering, fire safety and operational decisions remain with your qualified project team.
