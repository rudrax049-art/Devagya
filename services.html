Services

One method, three segments.

Individuals building a home. Companies planning a workplace. Developers laying out a project at scale. The method stays the same — what changes is the scope.

For Individuals

Residential Vastu & Spatial Design

What it is: A Vastu and spatial review for a new build, under-construction home, or existing property — covering entry orientation, room placement and daylight.

What's examined: Plot direction and entry, room-by-room placement, sun-path and natural light, and structural layout where plans are available.

What you receive: A written assessment with clear, practical recommendations you can hand to your architect or contractor.

How tradition and modern design integrate: Vastu placement principles are checked against real sun-path and airflow data for your specific site — not applied as generic rules.

Request this service

For Companies

Corporate & Workplace Vastu

What it is: A review of an office or workplace layout, balancing Vastu guidance with how a team actually works.

What's examined: Entry and reception placement, desk and seating zones, meeting-room location, and daylight/airflow across the floor.

What you receive: A layout review with specific, implementable adjustments — not a rebuild of your entire floor plan unless that's what's needed.

How tradition and modern design integrate: Directional principles are weighed against practical workplace function — collaboration zones, focus areas, and circulation.

Request this service

For Developers

Developer & Multi-Unit Planning

What it is: Vastu-integrated review of floor plans and multi-unit layouts at the planning stage — before units are finalised.

What's examined: Building orientation, unit-by-unit entry and room placement, and shared spaces across a floor or across multiple floors.

What you receive: A planning-stage report flagging adjustments while they're still inexpensive to make, backed by site and structural analysis.

How tradition and modern design integrate: Traditional principles are applied at the scale of a building or project, not just a single unit, alongside real structural and site constraints.

Request this service

Signature Spaces

A space designed around a specific identity or purpose, rather than a generic layout — for a home, an office, or a development's shared spaces. This sits on top of any of the three services above, for clients who want the space to feel distinct.
