The 45-zone grid

Forty-five points of reference.
One complete plan.

A plan can be overlaid with a nine-by-five analytical grid representing 45 traditional spatial zones. Our design review uses the framework to prompt practical questions about use, access, light, privacy and building constraints.

Grid positions are schematic. Accurate mapping requires a correctly oriented drawing and confirmed site information.
