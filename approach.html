The classical 45-zone framework provides a structured way to discuss orientation and room relationships. We use it as a planning lens alongside architectural, structural and code review.

The 45-zone grid

Forty-five points of reference.
One complete plan.

A plan can be overlaid with a nine-by-five analytical grid representing 45 distinct classical architectural deity energy nodes. These names belong to a traditional spatial framework; our design review translates the overlay into practical questions about use, access, light, privacy and building constraints.

Grid positions are schematic. Accurate mapping requires a correctly oriented drawing and confirmed site information.
