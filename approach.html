Methodology / Spatial analysis

A spatial framework, read through the plan.

Devagya Vastu provides spatial planning for homes, workplaces and developments, considering traditional Vastu frameworks alongside architecture. The 45 Vastu Devta fields provide named traditional references for discussing room use, access, daylight, privacy and design priorities.

The 45 Vastu Devta fields

Named traditional references.
Read with the full plan.

This reference groups Brahma at the centre, 12 inner and diagonal fields, and 32 outer-ring fields. It helps organise a design conversation around use, access, light, privacy and building constraints.

Names, transliterations and exact field placements vary among Vastu texts and lineages. This grouped reference is not a directional map; accurate mapping requires an oriented drawing and an agreed mandala tradition.
