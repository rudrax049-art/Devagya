Privacy

How this website handles your information.

Devagya Vastu uses information submitted through website forms to respond to the request made. This page explains the information handled by forms and local planning tools in plain language.

Information you submit

When you submit a consultation, scheduling, project-brief or guide-interest form, the fields you choose to provide are sent to Web3Forms for delivery to Devagya Vastu. Form data is not sent until you explicitly submit the form. Contact details may be used to respond to your enquiry.

Files and project drawings

The contact form does not upload selected drawings or files. If you choose files in the browser, they remain on your device; only file names may be included in the enquiry. Share drawings separately only after discussing a suitable channel.

Planning tools and local storage

The readiness checklist, project brief draft and language preference may be saved in your browser's local storage so you can return to them. These values stay on the device unless you choose to include information in a form or WhatsApp message. Use each tool's clear/reset control to remove its saved draft, or clear this site's storage in your browser settings.

Email updates

This website does not operate an email newsletter or automated update list. The project enquiry form has a separate, optional checkbox for occasional updates; if selected, that preference is sent with the enquiry so Devagya Vastu can follow up accordingly. You can withdraw that preference by emailing devagyavastu@gmail.com. Other enquiry details are used to respond to the request you made.

Third-party services

Form submissions are processed by Web3Forms, and visitors may choose to open WhatsApp or their email/phone application. Those services have their own privacy practices. The site exposes local custom events for consultation CTA clicks, form submissions and tool completions, but no analytics provider is configured and these events are not sent to a third party. No analytics or advertising cookies are used.

Website information and limits

Site content and interactive tools are general planning aids, not certified Vastu, engineering, architectural, safety, legal or code-compliance assessments. Project-specific decisions should be reviewed with qualified professionals responsible for the work.
