Privacy

How this website handles your information.

Devagya Vastu uses information submitted through website forms to respond to the request made. This page explains the information handled by forms and local planning tools in plain language.

Information you submit

Consultation forms prepare a message in your browser. The details you enter are not sent to Devagya Vastu until you choose to open WhatsApp and send the message. Contact details may then be used to respond to your enquiry.

Files and project drawings

Consultation forms do not collect or upload files. To share plans or photographs, open the prepared WhatsApp conversation and attach the files directly in that chat.

Planning tools and local storage

The readiness checklist and language preference may be saved in your browser's local storage. Campaign source values (UTM parameters) are saved in session storage and can be appended to a WhatsApp enquiry when you choose to open WhatsApp. These values stay on the device unless you choose to include information in a WhatsApp message. Use each tool's clear/reset control to remove saved progress, or clear this site's storage in your browser settings.

Email updates

This website does not operate an email newsletter or automated update list. Enquiry details are used to respond to the request you made.

Third-party services

Visitors may choose to open WhatsApp, which has its own privacy practices. The site prepares custom events for WhatsApp enquiries and tool use in a GA4-compatible data layer. No Google Analytics measurement ID is currently configured, so these events are not sent to Google. No analytics or advertising cookies are used.

Website information and limits

Site content and interactive tools are general planning aids, not certified Vastu, engineering, architectural, safety, legal or code-compliance assessments. Project-specific decisions should be reviewed with qualified professionals responsible for the work.
