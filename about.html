About Devagya Vastu: our story & philosophy.

Devagya Vastu exists on the idea that traditional spatial wisdom and contemporary design aren't competing systems — they're two lenses that, applied together, produce a more complete read of a space.

For generations, Vastu Shastra has guided how a home should meet the sun, how a threshold should be placed, how a room should hold the people living in it. We start every project there — with a plot, a direction, and that older discipline of reading a space before building on it.

We then test that reading against measurable factors: sun-path and daylight study, airflow and structural realities. Where tradition and measurement agree, we build with confidence. Where they pull in different directions, we say so and work through it with the client rather than staying quiet about the tension.

We also draw on environmental and positive psychology as a design lens — considering how comfort, privacy, light and clarity can support everyday experience. We're careful with this: it's a design consideration, not a promise about anyone's wellbeing or life outcomes.

Old order. New proof. New form.
